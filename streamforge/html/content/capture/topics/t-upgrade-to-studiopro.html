Upgrading to Studio Pro

Move from StreamForge Capture's simple layout to StreamForge Studio Pro's full scene, filter, and multi-camera toolset.

StreamForge Studio Pro includes everything in StreamForge Capture, plus unlimited scenes, filters, multi-camera switching, advanced overlays, and team collaboration. Upgrading does not require reinstalling—Studio Pro reads your existing Capture layout on first launch.

  1. Sign in to your StreamForge account and confirm your subscription includes the Pro tier.
    See Account and Licensing.
  2. Install StreamForge Studio Pro alongside, or in place of, StreamForge Capture.
  3. Launch Studio Pro and sign in with the same account.
Your existing Webcam, Microphone, and Screen sources appear in a new scene, and the full Pro feature set becomes available.
