Scenes and Sources

StreamForge Capture's simplified scene and source model.

A scene is a named collection of one or more sources—windows, cameras, images, or other on-screen elements—arranged together on a canvas. Switching scenes changes everything visible on screen at once, instead of repositioning sources one at a time.

In short: sources are the pieces, a scene is the arrangement.

StreamForge Capture deliberately supports only three source types—Webcam, Microphone, and Screen—compared to OBS Studio's eleven. There are no filters and no scripting; sources are added, resized, and arranged, and that's the whole model.

Capture also supports only one scene at a time rather than a saved collection of scenes, since its target use case is a single always-the-same webcam layout rather than switching between multiple setups.
