Remote Camera Control

Use your phone as a camera source and control it remotely from the touch interface.

Once paired, StreamForge Mobile can act as a wireless camera source, and its touch controls let you adjust the shot without touching the phone itself once it's mounted.

  1. On the phone, tap Start Camera.
  2. Use the on-screen pinch gesture to zoom, and drag to pan within the sensor's field of view.
  3. Optional: Tap the focus reticle on a subject to lock focus and exposure to that point.
The paired desktop product receives the video feed as a live camera source that updates as you adjust framing from the phone.
