Applying a Chroma Key Filter

Remove a solid-color background from a camera source.

A single, evenly lit solid-color backdrop behind the subject.
  1. Right-click the camera source and choose Filters.
  2. Under Video Filters, click + and choose Chroma Key.
  3. Set Key Color Type to match your backdrop color.
  4. Adjust Similarity and Smoothness until the background disappears cleanly without eating into the subject.
The backdrop becomes transparent, letting whatever is in scenes beneath it show through.
